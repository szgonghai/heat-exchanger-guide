## 🧪 Selection Decision (4 Steps)

### Step 1 · Media Corrosivity
| Media | Selection |
|-------|-----------|
| Water / aqueous solutions | Plate (SRP) / Spiral (KUROSE) / Shell-Plate (VAHTERUS) |
| Strong acid / strong base / strong corrosion | PTFE (WOOAM) |
| VOCs / waste gas | VOCs Condenser (COOL+) |

### Step 2 · Particles / Fouling
| Condition | Selection |
|-----------|-----------|
| Clean media | Plate (high efficiency) |
| Particles / fouling | Spiral (self-cleaning) / Shell-Plate |
| High viscosity | Spiral |

### Step 3 · Temperature / Pressure
| Range | Selection |
|-------|-----------|
| < 100°C, < 10 bar | Plate / PTFE |
| < 200°C, < 25 bar | Plate / Spiral / PTFE |
| < 250°C, < 30 bar | Shell-Plate |

### Step 4 · Special Requirements
- **Pharma / Food** → SUS316L polished
- **Electronic-grade** → PTFE
- **Marine certification** → Shell-Plate (VAHTERUS)

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Q1: Plate vs Spiral Heat Exchanger?</b></summary>

**Plate**: High efficiency and compact, suitable for clean media. **Spiral**: Self-cleaning, fouling resistant, suitable for particle-containing / fouling-prone / high-viscosity media. Standard industry → plate; special conditions → spiral.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Q2: Why are PTFE heat exchangers so expensive?</b></summary>

PTFE material resists strong acid/alkali, high temperature, and has no metal ion leaching — particularly suited for semiconductor / electronic-grade chemicals / pharmaceutical applications requiring extreme purity. Cost reflects material and manufacturing process.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Q3: How often to clean heat exchangers?</b></summary>

Depends on media: clean water 6~12 months; chemical media 1~3 months; particle/fouling media need backflush systems. Gonghai can provide cleaning cycle and spare parts plan.
</details>
