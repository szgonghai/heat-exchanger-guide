## Q2: How to select the right heat exchanger?

**A:** Selection depends on 4 key factors:

1. **Medium** — Corrosive media → PTFE; High viscosity / solids → Spiral; Water-oil → Plate
2. **Temperature** — Above 200°C → Shell & Plate; Below 200°C → Plate / Spiral
3. **Pressure** — Above 20 bar → Shell & Plate
4. **Cleanliness** — Semiconductor / high purity required → PTFE

## Q3: What is the difference between plate and shell & plate heat exchangers?

**A:** 

| | Plate (SRP) | Shell & Plate (VAHTERUS) |
|--|------------|--------------------------|
| Efficiency | Higher | Medium |
| Max Pressure | ~10-25 bar | ~25-30 bar |
| Max Temp | ~200°C | ~250°C |
| Temp Fluctuation | Medium | Strong tolerance |
| Maintenance | Easy (openable) | Medium |

---

## Q4: Can heat exchangers be used with corrosive media?

**A:** Yes. For strongly corrosive media (strong acids, alkalis, organic solvents), **PTFE heat exchangers (WOOAM)** are recommended. PTFE offers near-universal chemical resistance and does not leach metal ions, making it ideal for semiconductor and electronics-grade chemical applications.

---

## Q5: What maintenance do spiral heat exchangers require?

**A:** Spiral heat exchangers have a **self-cleaning design** — the spiral channel creates centrifugal force that flushes out solids and prevents fouling. However, periodic inspection is still recommended for:
- Checking for unexpected corrosion
- Verifying gasket integrity
- Monitoring pressure drop changes

---

- Chemical manufacturing (solvent recovery)
- Printing and coating (ink solvent recovery)
- Pharmaceutical (organic solvent recovery)

Recovery rate depends on inlet concentration and target condensation temperature.
